# VibeStudio Audience Retention & Engagement Insights (DOC-INSIGHTS-03)

## 1. Historical Retention Benchmarks (Q3–Q4 Telemetry)
- **Top-Performing Visual Styles:**
  - *Cozy Solarpunk & Isometric Tech Dioramas:* **84% 24-second completion rate** (highest share rate among developer and creator audiences).
  - *Cinematic Noir / Warm Amber Studio Lighting:* **79% completion rate** (strongest engagement for AI & engineering topics).
  - *Generic Stock Office Footage:* **31% completion rate** (avoid sterile corporate conference rooms).

## 2. Audio & Pacing Learnings
- Voiceover scripts exceeding **60 total words across 24 seconds** sound rushed and drop viewer retention by 22%. Keep each 8-second scene between **12 and 22 words**.
- Pairing concrete visual metaphors (e.g., a glowing railway switchboard representing an ADK Router node, or a brass vault door representing a Human-in-the-Loop gate) boosts rewatch rate by **1.8x**.
