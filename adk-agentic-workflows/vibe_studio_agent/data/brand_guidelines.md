# VibeStudio Enterprise Brand & Safety Guidelines (DOC-BRAND-01)

## 1. Visual & Brand Identity
- **Channel Persona:** VibeStudio produces high-craft, cinematic short-form explainer and storytelling videos (24 seconds total across 3 scenes).
- **Color & Lighting Standard:** Every scene prompt must specify explicit lighting and camera movement (e.g., `"warm tungsten key light, slow dolly-in"` or `"soft volumetric dusk lighting, orbital pan"`).
- **Character & Prop Continuity:** Maintain consistent visual anchors across all 3 scenes (same protagonist attire, color palette, and lens focal length such as `35mm anamorphic`).

## 2. Prohibited Content & Banned Tropes (Strict Policy Gate)
Storyboards containing any of the following banned phrases or visual tropes **must be rejected** by the Policy Critic before Human-in-the-Loop review or Veo rendering:
- `flashing strobe` or `rapid flickering lights` (accessibility & photosensitive epilepsy hazard)
- `guaranteed profit` or `get rich quick` (financial compliance violation)
- `gore`, `blood`, or `explicit violence` (platform safety violation)
- `unlicensed celebrity likeness` or `copyrighted logo` (IP compliance violation)
- `clickbait shock face` (brand quality violation)

## 3. Production Budget Guardrails
- **Standard Scene Cost:** Each 8-second Veo video scene + Imagen 3 reference keyframe costs **$3.50 USD** ($10.50 USD for a standard 3-scene 24-second short).
- **Maximum Auto-Render Budget Cap:** **$15.00 USD** per video run. Any storyboard exceeding 4 scenes or $15.00 USD must be trimmed back to 3 scenes (24 seconds).
